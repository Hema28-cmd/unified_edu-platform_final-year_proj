Social Studies - Class 12

Advanced topics: History, Geography, Political Science & Economics

History: Modern India & World History

Study India’s independence movements, post-independence challenges, partition, and contemporary global events. Learn world history including world wars, revolutions, and major socio-political changes in the 20th century.

Geography: Human & Economic Geography

Explore population distribution, urbanization, agriculture, industries, globalization, trade, and development issues. Learn about environmental management, climate change, and sustainable practices worldwide.

Political Science: Contemporary India

Understand the Indian political system, governance challenges, constitutional amendments, social movements, foreign policy, and the role of media. Study democracy, federalism, and public policy in depth.

Economics: Indian & Global Economy

Advanced economic concepts including macroeconomic policies, development indicators, economic reforms, international trade, globalization, fiscal and monetary policy, and economic planning in India and globally.

Current Affairs & Contemporary Issues

Keep updated on recent national and international events, climate action, social justice issues, economic developments, policy decisions, and global trends impacting society.

Projects & Research Activities

Engage in research projects, case studies, surveys, debates, model making, presentations, and eco-friendly initiatives. Promote analytical thinking, teamwork, and practical application of social studies concepts.
