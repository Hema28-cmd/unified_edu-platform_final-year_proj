Social Studies - Class 11

Explore History, Geography, Political Science, and Economics in detail

History: Early Modern India

Study the Mughal Empire, regional kingdoms, British colonization, resistance movements, and social-cultural changes. Focus on the evolution of Indian society and economy during this period.

Geography: Physical & Human

Learn about landforms, climate, vegetation, water resources, population patterns, urbanization, and human-environment interaction. Explore sustainable development concepts and regional resources.

Political Science: Indian Constitution

Understand the Constitution of India, fundamental rights and duties, governance, federalism, elections, political parties, and contemporary political challenges in India.

Economics: Micro & Macro

Study demand, supply, market structures, national income, inflation, fiscal and monetary policies, development economics, and apply concepts to real-world scenarios.

Current Affairs

Keep updated with national and international events, policy changes, social issues, global economic trends, and analyze their impact on society and governance.

Projects & Activities

Engage in debates, map work, surveys, role-plays, model building, and eco-friendly initiatives. Enhance critical thinking, teamwork, and applied knowledge in practical contexts.
