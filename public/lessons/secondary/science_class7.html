Science – Class 7

📖 Topic: Cells – The Basic Unit of Life

Cells are the smallest unit of life and the building blocks of all living organisms.

Main Components:

  • Nucleus – Control center of the cell
  • Cell Membrane – Protects and encloses the cell
  • Cytoplasm – Gel-like substance where organelles reside

Example:

Plant cells have a rigid cell wall and chloroplasts for photosynthesis.

✅ This lesson helps students understand the structure and function of cells.
