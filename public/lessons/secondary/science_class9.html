📖 Topic: Newton's Laws of Motion

Newton’s Laws explain the relationship between a body and the forces acting on it.

The Three Laws:

  • 1st Law (Inertia) – A body remains at rest or in uniform motion unless acted on by a force
  • 2nd Law – Force = Mass × Acceleration (F = ma)
  • 3rd Law – For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction

Example:

Pushing a stationary ball requires force; the ball accelerates based on mass.

✅ This lesson helps students understand the fundamentals of motion and forces.
