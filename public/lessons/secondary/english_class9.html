📖 Topic: Active and Passive Voice

Voice shows whether the subject performs or receives the action in a sentence.

Main Types:

  • Active Voice – Subject performs the action
  • Passive Voice – Subject receives the action

Example:

The teacher explains the lesson. (Active)

The lesson is explained by the teacher. (Passive)

✅ This lesson helps students improve writing style and clarity.
