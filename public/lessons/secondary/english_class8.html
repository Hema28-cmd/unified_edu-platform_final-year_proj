📖 Topic: Tenses

Tenses indicate the time of an action or state of being in a sentence.

Main Types of Tenses:

  • Present Tense – Action happening now
  • Past Tense – Action happened before
  • Future Tense – Action will happen

Example:

She is reading a book. (Present)

✅ This lesson helps students use correct tenses in writing and speaking.
