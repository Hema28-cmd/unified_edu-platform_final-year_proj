📖 Topic: Human Digestive System

The digestive system helps the body break down food into nutrients for energy and growth.

Main Organs:

  • Mouth – Chews food and mixes it with saliva
  • Stomach – Breaks down food with enzymes and acids
  • Small Intestine – Absorbs nutrients
  • Large Intestine – Absorbs water and forms waste

Example:

Food travels from the mouth → esophagus → stomach → intestines.

✅ This lesson helps students understand how the human body processes food.
